08 · Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you're
wondering, answered.

What is the duration of the programme?+
Eleven months, full-time and on-campus in Chennai. The Class of 2027 batch starts January 2027, and applications close November 30 · or earlier if the cohort fills.
Who can apply for this programme?+
Anyone 18 or older. A bachelor's degree is welcomed but not required, selection is based on passion and potential, not pedigree.
Is prior filmmaking experience required?+
No. Share anything you've made, a phone film, photos, writing, and a short statement of intent. No prior work? Tell us what you'd make.
What kind of films will I make?+
Six films across the year: a photo story, a continuity exercise, an interview exercise, a complete documentary, a music video / ad film, and your 10-minute final narrative short, premiered in a Qube-equipped theatre.
Where is the campus located?+
On the 30-acre Thandalam campus in Chennai, in partnership with Rajalakshmi Institutions (NAAC A++), real buildings, courtyards and grounds that become your sets, with edit & DI suites and a cinema theatre on campus.
What is the admission process?+
Four steps: apply online, share your portfolio and statement of intent, a relaxed conversation with our faculty, then your offer and enrolment. Our team will reach out within 24 hours.
What are the fees, and are installments available?+
The one-year programme fee is ₹5,50,000 for the year, inclusive of equipment and campus production resources. Instalment / payment plans are available — our admissions team will walk you through the options on your call.
What career support will I get?+
A 1:3 faculty ratio of working professionals, masterclasses from the advisory board, industry connections through Qube Cinema and LevelUp Learning, your thesis film distributed to industry, and personalised placement guidance.
