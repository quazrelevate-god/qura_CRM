QURA Film Academy ("QURA", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This page explains, in plain terms, what we collect when you enquire or apply through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

When you submit an application or enquiry form, we collect the details you provide — your name, WhatsApp number, email, city and state, and your answers about your background, goals and interest in the programme. We also record basic technical information such as the page you submitted from and standard advertising identifiers used to measure our campaigns.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and guide you through admissions — by phone, WhatsApp and email.
  • To assess your fit for the programme and arrange the entrance exam and interview.
  • To measure and improve our marketing, including through Meta and Google advertising tools.

Sharing

We share your details only with our admissions and operations team and the service providers that help us run admissions and communications. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

You can ask us to stop contacting you, or to access or delete your information, at any time by writing to admissions@qurafilm.com.
