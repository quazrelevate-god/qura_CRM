The Curriculum · 4 Phases

Built the way films are made.

Months 1–2

Foundations

Still photography, composition, framing. The language of cinema and visual grammar. You learn to see like a filmmaker before you touch a movie camera.

Months 3–5

Building Blocks

Moving image, continuity, dialogue. Documentary theory and interview craft. Cameras, sound recording and editing software become daily tools in your hands.

Months 6–8

Applied Craft

Documentary, music video and ad filmmaking, including AI-assisted production tools, plus advanced post-production. Your highest period of creative output.

Months 9–11

The Final Film

Write, shoot and complete your 10-minute narrative short, your thesis film, screened in a Qube-equipped theatre and taken into the industry.
