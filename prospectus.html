From the Founder

A letter from
Lata Murugan.

I grew up in the corridors of AVM Studios — India’s oldest surviving film studio, founded by my grandfather, A. V. Meiyappan. Cinema was not a career choice for me; it was the air I breathed. But when I entered film education — first as a student, then as a teacher across India, the United States and Southern Africa — I saw the same gap everywhere.

Film schools taught craft in silos. You chose direction or cinematography or editing in your first year, before you understood what filmmaking truly demanded. Theory lived in one classroom; practice lived in another. And the cost of entry locked talented students from middle-class families out entirely.

QURA is my answer to all of that.

In twenty years of teaching — at L.V. Prasad Film & TV Academy, at institutions across three continents — I have seen what produces great filmmakers. It is not specialisation at eighteen. It is immersion: making films from day one, learning every craft, and discovering your voice through the act of creation itself.

That is what we have built at QURA. An eleven-month intensive where you don’t just study filmmaking — you do it. Six films. Four phases. Theory every morning, production every afternoon. And at ₹5.5 lakhs, a price that keeps this education within reach of talent, not just means.

Our faculty are not academics who once made films. They are working filmmakers — Emmy-nominated cinematographers, Netflix-credited editors, Fulbright scholars — who bring the industry into the classroom every single day. Our partners — Qube Cinema, Rajalakshmi Institutions and LevelUp Learning — bring technology, infrastructure and a community of 18,000+ filmmakers to your doorstep.

To the students reading this: if you have a story to tell, we will give you the craft to tell it. To the parents reading this: your child will be safe, supervised, and shaped by people who have dedicated their lives to this art form.

Warm regards,
Lata Murugan

03 · The Curriculum — Four Phases

Built the way films are made.

Months
1 – 2
Foundations

Still photography, composition, framing. The language of cinema and visual grammar. You learn to see like a filmmaker before you touch a movie camera — and tell your first story through curated still images.

Months
3 – 5
Building Blocks

Moving image, continuity, dialogue. Documentary theory and interview craft. Cameras, sound recording and editing software become daily tools in your hands.

Months
6 – 8
Applied Craft

Documentary, music video and ad filmmaking — including AI-assisted production tools — plus advanced post-production. Your highest period of creative output.

Months
9 – 11
The Final Film

Write, shoot and complete your 10-minute narrative short — your thesis film. Faculty mentorship intensifies. Your film premieres in a Qube-equipped theatre before an industry audience, and the best work is submitted to national and international festivals.

04

18 and above. A bachelor’s degree is welcomed but not required — we admit on passion and potential, not pedigree.

A point of view. People who can’t stop thinking about film — and have something they want to say with it.

Commitment. A full-time, on-campus programme in Chennai. You’ll live and breathe filmmaking for eleven months.

No film background?

Many of our strongest applicants come in self-taught — a phone, a DSLR, an obsession. We teach the craft from foundations up.
