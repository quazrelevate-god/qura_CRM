FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need a film background to apply?

No. Many of our strongest applicants come in self-taught, a phone, a DSLR, an obsession. We teach the craft from foundations up.

Is the programme full-time and on-campus?

Yes. It's a full-time, on-campus programme in Chennai across our City Campus and the 30-acre Thandalam film city.

What do I graduate with?

A portfolio of six completed films, a certification by the Rajalakshmi Group (NAAC A++), and a network of industry mentors, plus the all-round command to work on any set.

I only want to study cinematography, why learn writing, direction and editing too?

Because the best cinematographers understand the whole film. Knowing how a scene is written, directed and cut makes you a sharper collaborator and a more employable professional, and it's how the masters on our advisory board began. You specialise deeply, but never in isolation.

Are scholarships available?

A limited number of scholarships are available, subject to eligibility. Please indicate your interest in the application and our team will be in touch.

When does the next batch start?

January 2027. Applications close November 30, 2026, or once the cohort is full, whichever is earlier.
