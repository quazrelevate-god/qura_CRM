§02 · The Weekly

[Thursday] night,
wherever you are.

Every week, without a break, the community meets online. No syllabus, no attendance, no certificate at the end of it. Someone brings a scene. Everyone else takes it apart.

It is the least glamorous thing we do and the reason the community is the size it is. You cannot fake having done this for years, and you cannot shortcut what it teaches you about your own work.

§03 · The Circles
